# DialTone.Med Email Sequence: Specialty Practice

For specialist practices such as oral surgery, dermatology, orthopedics, cardiology, and GI
(CRM business type **Specialty Practice**). Send to the contact on the lead.

Placeholders: `[first_name]`, `[practice_name]`, `[specialty]` (for example "oral surgery"), `[Your Name]`,
`[Day/Time A]`, `[Day/Time B]`.

## Email 1: First Touch (Day 1)

**Subject options:**

- `referrals that ring out`
- `phones at [practice_name]`
- `"is my authorization approved yet?"`

> Hi [first_name],
>
> For a [specialty] practice, a missed call is often a referral, a patient who was sent to you and called the
> next office on the list instead.
>
> DialTone.Med answers every call to [practice_name], checks who's calling, and texts a secure link to finish
> the request: book the consult, pay a balance, update insurance. Anything clinical goes to your staff in the
> patient's own words.
>
> Would a 15-minute demo be worth it? You'd hear it handle a patient call.
>
> [Your Name]
> DialTone.Med

## Email 2: Prior-Auth Angle (Day 4)

**Subject:** `re: [the subject of Email 1]`

> Hi [first_name],
>
> One more thought. After a procedure is scheduled, the phones fill up with "has my authorization come
> through yet?"
>
> Our agent answers those calls with the status only, never the procedure, and texts the details. The status
> comes from AuthPulse, our prior-auth tracker, which is in early access. Every request gets a deadline clock
> where a federal timeframe applies, and it flags late decisions, denials sent without a specific reason, and
> approvals about to expire.
>
> How does [practice_name] track authorization deadlines today?
>
> [Your Name]

## Email 3: Close the Loop (Day 9)

**Subject:** `re: [the subject of Email 1]`

> Hi [first_name],
>
> I'll stop here. If phones and prior auths aren't a priority right now, reply "not now" and I'll check back
> in a few months.
>
> If they are, I can walk you through it on [Day/Time A] or [Day/Time B].
>
> [Your Name]

## After a Call: Voicemail Follow-Up

**Subject:** `just left you a voicemail`

> Hi [first_name],
>
> I just left a message at [practice_name]. Short version: DialTone.Med answers every patient call, including
> referrals and prior-auth status questions, and texts a secure link to finish the request.
>
> Would [Day/Time A] or [Day/Time B] work for a 15-minute demo?
>
> [Your Name]

## After a Call: "Send Me Information"

**Subject:** `DialTone.Med for [practice_name], as promised`

> Hi [first_name],
>
> Thanks for taking my call. Here's the short version.
>
> DialTone.Med answers every call, verifies the patient, and texts a secure link that finishes the request:
>
> - **Prior-auth status:** status only on the call; details and next steps by secure link, and a text when it changes.
> - **Appointments:** consults and follow-ups booked, rescheduled, or cancelled from the link.
> - **Billing:** the patient pays their portion by card from the link. Nothing is said aloud.
> - **Insurance:** the patient enters new plan details, and your staff review the eligibility result.
> - **Clinical questions and refills:** taken in the patient's own words and queued for your team.
>
> **AuthPulse** (early access) tracks every prior authorization your team submits: when each answer is due,
> which are late, which denials need a second look, and which approvals expire soon. It doesn't submit or
> decide anything; your team keeps doing that.
>
> Does [Day/Time A] or [Day/Time B] work for 15 minutes?
>
> [Your Name]

## Notes

- Send Email 2 and 3 only if there's no reply. Stop as soon as anyone answers, including "no."
- Mark **Emailed** in the CRM after Email 1, and log replies in the notes.
